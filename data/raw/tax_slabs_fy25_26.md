# Income Tax Slabs FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27)

Source: https://cleartax.in/s/income-tax-slabs

## New Tax Regime Slabs (FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27)
The new tax regime is the default tax regime and offers lower tax rates with a higher basic exemption limit of Rs. 4 lakh.

| Income Slab | Tax Rate |
| :--- | :--- |
| Up to Rs. 4,00,000 | Nil |
| Rs. 4,00,001 to Rs. 8,00,000 | 5% |
| Rs. 8,00,001 to Rs. 12,00,000 | 10% |
| Rs. 12,00,001 to Rs. 16,00,000 | 15% |
| Rs. 16,00,001 to Rs. 20,00,000 | 20% |
| Rs. 20,00,001 to Rs. 24,00,000 | 25% |
| Above Rs. 24,00,000 | 30% |

**Note:** Salaried individuals are eligible for a standard deduction of Rs. 75,000. Additionally, a full tax rebate under Section 87A is available for taxable income up to Rs. 12 lakh, making it effectively tax-free.

## Old Tax Regime Slabs (FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27)
The old tax regime allows for various deductions (like 80C, 80D, HRA) but has lower basic exemption limits.

### 1. Individuals (Below 60 years of age)
| Income Slab | Tax Rate |
| :--- | :--- |
| Up to Rs. 2,50,000 | Nil |
| Rs. 2,50,001 to Rs. 5,00,000 | 5% |
| Rs. 5,00,001 to Rs. 10,00,000 | 20% |
| Above Rs. 10,00,000 | 30% |

### 2. Senior Citizens (Aged 60 to 80 years)
| Income Slab | Tax Rate |
| :--- | :--- |
| Up to Rs. 3,00,000 | Nil |
| Rs. 3,00,001 to Rs. 5,00,000 | 5% |
| Rs. 5,00,001 to Rs. 10,00,000 | 20% |
| Above Rs. 10,00,000 | 30% |

### 3. Super Senior Citizens (Aged 80 years and above)
| Income Slab | Tax Rate |
| :--- | :--- |
| Up to Rs. 5,00,000 | Nil |
| Rs. 5,00,001 to Rs. 10,00,000 | 20% |
| Above Rs. 10,00,000 | 30% |

**Note:** A tax rebate under Section 87A is available in the old regime for taxable income up to Rs. 5 lakh (rebate of up to Rs. 12,500). Health and Education Cess of 4% is applicable on the total income tax across both regimes. Surcharge is also applicable for high-income earners.
